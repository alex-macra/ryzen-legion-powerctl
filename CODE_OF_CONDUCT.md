# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a small hardware-tuning project; discussion should stay about
power limits, kernels, and laptops.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most "wrong" reports are someone with different firmware.
- Be specific and factual in issues: model, CPU, kernel, RyzenAdj version,
  `legion-powerctl doctor` output.
- Accept that maintainer time is limited and that hardware nobody owns cannot be
  supported directly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, or publishing others' private information.

Also unacceptable here specifically: encouraging people to run values you know
to be unsafe for their hardware, or presenting untested settings as verified.
This project writes low-level power limits; overconfidence has physical
consequences on someone else's machine.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer at mail@alexmacra.com.
Reports are handled confidentially. For security vulnerabilities use the process
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments and may block accounts that do
not follow this document.
